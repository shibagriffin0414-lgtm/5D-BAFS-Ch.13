Ch.13

Financial Statements for Partnerships

BAFS Ch 13

13.2 Partnership Agreement

To prevent conflicts, partners agree on major issues before starting a business. Key items include:

  • Capital contribution: Amount agreed to be contributed (not necessarily equal).
  • Profit and loss sharing ratio: How P&L is shared (can link to capital/workload).
  • Rate of interest on capital: Minimum return on capital to reward higher contributors.
  • Rate of interest on drawings: Discourages partners from taking resources out.
  • Salaries to partners: Rewards partners who contribute more time and effort.

Partnership Ordinance (Chapter 38)

If there is NO agreement, default rules apply:

  • Profits/losses shared equally
  • No interest on capital
  • No interest on drawings
  • No partner salaries
  • 8% p.a. interest on advances (loans) from partners

13.3 Profit and Loss Appropriation

Net profit is not shared immediately. It is appropriated via the Appropriation Account.

Appropriation Account Format
Net Profit for the periodXXX
Add: Interest on drawingsXX
Less: Interest on capital(XX)
Less: Salaries to partners(XX)
= Balance of profit/loss to be sharedXXX

Capital vs. Current Accounts

Fluctuating Capital Accounts

One single account per partner.

  • Records ALL items (Capital, Drawings, Interests, Salaries, Profit Share)

Fixed Capital Accounts

Two separate accounts per partner.

  • Capital Account: ONLY records capital contributed/withdrawn.
  • Current Account: Records recurring items (Drawings, Interests, Salaries, Profit).

13.4 Special Items

Salaries Treatment

Staff Salaries: Treated as an Operating Expense.

Partner Salaries: Treated as an Appropriation of profit.

Partner Loans

The Loan: Treated as a Liability.

Interest on Loan: Treated as an Expense (NOT an appropriation).
