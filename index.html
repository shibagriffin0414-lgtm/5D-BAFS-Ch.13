13.2 Partnership Agreement

To prevent conflicts, partners agree on major issues before starting a business. Key items include:

  • Capital contribution: Amount agreed to be contributed (not necessarily equal).
  • Profit and loss sharing ratio: How P&L is shared. Can be linked to capital or workload.
  • Rate of interest on capital: Minimum return on capital to reward those who contribute more.
  • Rate of interest on drawings: Discourages partners from taking resources out of the firm.
  • Salaries to partners: Rewards partners who contribute more time and effort.
Partnership Ordinance (Chapter 38) If there is no agreement: Profits/losses are shared equally, no interest on capital/drawings, no partner salaries, and 8% p.a. interest on advances (loans) from partners.

13.3 Profit and Loss Appropriation

Net profit is not shared immediately. It is appropriated via the Appropriation Account:

Appropriation Account
Net Profit for the period XXX
+ Interest on drawings XX
- Interest on capital (XX)
- Salaries to partners (XX)
= Balance of profit/loss to be shared XXX

Capital vs. Current Accounts

Fluctuating Capital Accounts

One account per partner. Records ALL items.

  • Capital contributed
  • Profit/Loss shared
  • Drawings
  • Interest on capital/drawings
  • Partner's salary

Fixed Capital Accounts

Two accounts per partner to separate capital from recurring items.

  • Capital Account: Only records capital contributed/withdrawn.
  • Current Account: Records recurring items (Drawings, Interests, Salaries, Profit share).

13.4 Special Items

Staff vs Partner Salaries

Staff: Expense (deducted before net profit).
Partner: Appropriation (deducted after net profit).

Loans from Partners

Treated as a liability (non-current or current).
Interest on Loan: Treated as an expense (not appropriation). Credited to Accrued Loan Interest if unpaid.
