HKDSE BAFS Chapter 13

Financial Statements for Partnerships

13.2 Partnership Agreement

To prevent conflicts, partners agree on major issues before starting a business. Key items include:

  • Capital contribution: Amount agreed to be contributed (not necessarily equal).
  • Profit and loss sharing ratio: How P&L is shared. Can be linked to capital or workload.
  • Rate of interest on capital: Minimum return on capital to reward those who contribute more.
  • Rate of interest on drawings: Discourages partners from taking resources out of the firm.
  • Salaries to partners: Rewards partners who contribute more time and effort.

Partnership Ordinance (Chapter 38)

If there is no agreement: Profits/losses are shared equally, no interest on capital/drawings, no partner salaries, and 8% p.a. interest on advances (loans) from partners.

13.3 Profit and Loss Appropriation

Net profit is not shared immediately. It is appropriated via the Appropriation Account:

Net Profit for the periodXXX
+ Interest on drawingsXX
- Interest on capital(XX)
- Salaries to partners(XX)
= Balance of profit/loss to be sharedXXX

Capital vs. Current Accounts

Fluctuating Capital Accounts

One account per partner. Records ALL items:

  • Capital contributed
  • Profit/Loss shared
  • Drawings
  • Interest on capital/drawings
  • Partner's salary

Fixed Capital Accounts

Separates investments from recurring items.

  • Capital Account: Only records capital contributed or withdrawn.
  • Current Account: Records all recurring items (profits, drawings, interest, salaries).
